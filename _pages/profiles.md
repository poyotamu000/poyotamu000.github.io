## JSK Robotics Laboratory and EVA Group

I am a **Project Assistant Professor** at the **JSK Robotics Laboratory, The University of Tokyo**.

My primary affiliation is JSK, where I lead and develop research on biomimetic robotics and constructive approaches to biological understanding.

I also conduct collaborative research with the **EVA (Embodied Vision and Action) group, The University of Tokyo**.
